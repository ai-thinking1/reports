How are they revered? The god vs human spectrum

Hindu traditions place figures on a spectrum: humble teachers and poet-saints; venerated sants with shrines (samadhi); figures worshipped as incarnations or avatars of deities; and a few proclaimed living God. Tags are heuristics from Wikipedia text signals, refined with cited research for major figures. They are comparative shorthand, not doctrine.
